# Support

Submarine is a free SSH, SFTP, RDP, VNC and Telnet client for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS. Here is where to get help.

## Questions, bugs and ideas

[Open an issue](https://github.com/SinaXhpm/Submarine/issues/new) on GitHub. For a bug, these help:

- the app's version and your system;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened;
- the lines from the tab's log, if there are any.

Issues are public, so please leave out passwords, keys, server addresses and anything else private.

## Downloads

The newest versions are on the [releases page](https://github.com/SinaXhpm/Submarine/releases).

## Your data and your account

The [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) says what is kept and for how long. To delete your sync account and everything the server holds for it, open **Cloud sync → Delete account…** in the app, or use the [delete account page](https://submarine.sinaxhpm.com/delete-account/).

## Security problems

Please don't post a security problem in a public issue. Write through [sinaxhpm.com](https://sinaxhpm.com) instead.
